The codes were run on an AMD Ryzen 9 5900X (12 cores, 128GB) under Linux. Given are total CPU seconds; maxtime = 40000s. For all codes error measures are given as defined in the 7th DIMACS Challenge benchmark paper Math Prog 95, 407-430 (2003), plato.asu.edu/dimacs/node3.html. For recent benchmarks of interest see SDP-GPU and cuHALLaR. (cu)HALLaR is not applicable to general SDP problems since it requires prior knowledge of a tracebound.
